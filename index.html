About Me

I am a Senior Software Engineering student at the University of Eastern Africa, Baraton, passionate about building innovative technology solutions and exploring the intersection of software engineering, artificial intelligence, machine learning, and data science. My academic training and hands-on exploration continue to strengthen my ability to approach complex problems analytically and transform ideas into practical digital solutions.

My technical foundation includes strong working proficiency in Python and JavaScript, alongside practical experience with HTML, CSS, databases, command-line environments, system troubleshooting, and software development. I am increasingly focused on using Python and data-driven approaches to explore data science, artificial intelligence, and machine learning.

I am particularly interested in how data, AI, and machine learning can be used to understand patterns, automate processes, support intelligent decision-making, and solve real-world problems. I am continuously developing my knowledge in these areas while building on my existing foundation in software architecture, system analysis, UI/UX, and digital product development.

I also serve as the GDG Data Science Track Lead at the University of Eastern Africa, Baraton, where I am interested in contributing to a community of learners and technology enthusiasts through knowledge sharing, collaboration, and exploration of data science, AI, machine learning, and emerging technologies.

Outside the technical space, I am deeply interested in music and nature, particularly singing, playing the piano, and nature walking. These interests strengthen my creativity, discipline, curiosity, and attention to detail—qualities I bring into my work and continuous learning journey.

My long-term objective is to become a highly skilled technology professional specializing in software engineering, artificial intelligence, machine learning, and data science, capable of designing impactful systems and using technology and data to create meaningful solutions to real-world challenges.
