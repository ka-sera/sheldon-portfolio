About Me

I am a Junior Software Engineering student at the University of Eastern Africa, Baraton, currently developing advanced competencies in software development, systems engineering, and modern computing technologies.

My technical foundation includes strong working proficiency in Python and JavaScript, together with practical experience in HTML and CSS, command-line environments, system troubleshooting, and software optimization.

I am particularly interested in software architecture, system design, UI/UX, and user-centered development. I approach engineering challenges analytically, focusing on scalable, reliable, and maintainable solutions.

Outside technology, I enjoy singing, playing the piano, and nature walking. These interests contribute to my creativity, discipline, and attention to detail.

My long-term objective is to become a highly skilled software engineer capable of designing impactful systems and contributing meaningfully to technological innovation.
